How a skill grows

Every one of Bloomy's skills is a ~25-minute journey through three stages — modeled on how mastery actually takes root.

Stage 01

The seed

Base Camp

Clear concept instruction with worked examples and think-aloud modeling, then a quick check for understanding.

Stage 02

The sprout

Climb

Adaptive guided practice that adjusts difficulty in real time — always in that sweet spot between too easy and too hard.

Stage 03

The bloom

An independent mastery assessment — no hints, no AI help. Score 90% or better and the skill is truly yours.

Meet BloomyBot

A tutor that asks,
never tells.

BloomyBot is a Socratic AI tutor. It never hands over the answer — it diagnoses the misconception, then walks students through structured scaffolding until the reasoning is theirs.

Diagnoses misconceptions

Finds the exact wrong turn in a student's thinking — not just the wrong answer.

Scaffolds the reasoning

Three levels of hints that guide students up — without carrying them.

Locked during Summit

Assessments are truly independent — and every conversation is visible to teachers.
