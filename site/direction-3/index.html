Meet BloomyBot

The tutor that
won't just tell you.

BloomyBot is Socratic to its core. It spots the exact wrong turn in a student's thinking, then asks the question that helps them find the way themselves. The answer earned beats the answer given — every time.

🔍 Diagnoses the misconception, not just the mistake
🪜 Scaffolds reasoning in structured steps
🔒 Locked during Summit — and every chat is teacher-visible
Maya, grade 5Is 3/8 bigger than 2/5? I said yes because 3 is bigger than 2.
BloomyBotInteresting! You compared the tops. What do the bottoms tell us about the size of each piece?
MayaOhhh… eighths are smaller pieces than fifths?
BloomyBotExactly. If both were cut into 40 pieces, how many pieces would each fraction be?↳ scaffold level 2 — still not telling!
Maya15/40 and 16/40… 2/5 wins. I had it backwards! 🎉
