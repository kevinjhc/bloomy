Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before requesting a demo.

Is Bloomy aligned to my state's standards?

Yes, every one of Bloomy's nearly 2,000 skills maps to Common Core or your state-specific framework, and question formats mirror your summative tests.

Does BloomyBot replace teacher instruction?

No. BloomyBot is a Socratic AI tutor that scaffolds reasoning during independent practice. It's disabled entirely during Summit assessments, and every conversation is logged for teacher visibility.

What subjects does Bloomy support?

English Language Arts (~700 skills across 6 domains) and Mathematics (~1,275 skills across 5 strands), covering grades K–12.

How does the 90% mastery threshold work?

Students must score 90% or better on an independent Summit assessment, with no hints and no AI support, before advancing. Fall short, and Bloomy re-teaches the concept a different way.

Is Bloomy accessible for all learners?

Yes, read-aloud across all content, adjustable dyslexia-friendly text sizing, full keyboard navigation, and complete screen-reader support are built in by default.
