✏️ How Bloomy works

Every skill is a little journey —
learn it, practice it, prove it.

About 25 minutes per skill, tuned to each student. No one moves on until the skill truly sticks.

Step one

Learn it

A clear, worked-example lesson with think-aloud modeling — then a quick check to make sure the idea landed.

Concept instruction ✓

Step two

Practice it

Guided practice that adapts in real time — three levels of gentle scaffolding keep it never too easy, never too hard.

Adaptive difficulty ✓

Step three

Prove it

An independent mastery check — no hints, no AI help. Score 90% or better and the skill is yours for good.

True mastery ✓
